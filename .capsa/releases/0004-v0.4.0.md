## Highlights

The five gaps 0.3.0 recorded as out of scope are closed. Interfaces (§4.11),
milestones (§4.12), release lines (§4.13) and platforms (§4.14) become
records; `scoped_status` (§2.5) carries facts that vary by line or platform;
`targets` (§2.6) makes a quantitative bar a field rather than a sentence.
Releases name their `line`; issues record per-line `fix_commits`, because a
backport is a different commit on each line.

Core 0.3.0 adds `aims_at` and `exposes` to the link vocabulary.

The umbrella tidy-up also completed in this cycle: the project format now
lives under `project/`, the validator under `tools/`, and the link schema
fragment under `core/` — a typed edge is core grammar, so keeping it under one
format would have implied the other needs a copy.

## Compatibility

Additive throughout. A 0.3.0 capsule conforms unchanged; `milestone` as a free
string on a plan is deprecated but still legal.

## Known gaps

The five identified gaps are closed, and no new ones were recorded in their
place. What remains open is not the format but its consumers: the neighbourhood
query, the derived index and the repair tool all live outside a capsule by
construction, and none of them exists yet.
