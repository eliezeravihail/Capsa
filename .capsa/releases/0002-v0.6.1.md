## Highlights

Inherits core v0.5.0 (§Checking): the validator checks conformance to the
grammar, never the truth of a conforming record — the same relationship a
PDF validator has to a PDF's content. `X-` is reserved for
operator-defined finding codes, so a client's own stricter enforcement
(whatever it wants to check beyond what Capsa itself can) composes with
the reference validator's findings instead of requiring a fork.

Documentation and governance only. No field changed, no existing check
changed behavior — PATCH on both `project/SPEC.md` (0.6.0 → 0.6.1) and
`organization/SPEC.md` (0.2.0 → 0.2.1).

## Notes

Follows directly from `examples/netron/.capsa/issues/
0003-regression-ref-presence-not-meaning.md` being rejected: the
rejection was correct about what the validator shouldn't do (verify
truth), but a follow-up question made the other half explicit — nothing
in Capsa should stop an operator from enforcing whatever it can, on top,
as strictly as it wants. §Checking and the `X-` convention are that: not
a retraction of the rejection, its completion.
