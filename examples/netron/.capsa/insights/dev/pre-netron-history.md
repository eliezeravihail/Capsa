`git log --reverse` shows the earliest commits ("adding tools", "adding
code", 2010-12-26) predate `package.json` (2017-11-16) and
`CITATION.cff`'s `date-released: 2017-12-04` by seven years. The project
existed under different branding before becoming "Netron" — `capsule.yaml`
and `releases/0001-v0.1.1.md` both anchor to the 2017 identity rather than
the 2010 origin, because that's what the project's own citation metadata
treats as authoritative, and because this capsule documents Netron, not
whatever preceded it under another name.
